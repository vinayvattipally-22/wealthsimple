# Employment Income and T4 Box Guide
Source: CRA T4 — Statement of Remuneration Paid, Employers' Guide T4001
Last Updated: 2024 Tax Year

## What is a T4 Slip?
The T4 — Statement of Remuneration Paid is issued by employers to report employment income and deductions for the tax year. Employers must issue T4 slips by the last day of February following the calendar year.

## T4 Box Reference Guide

### Income Boxes
| Box | Description | Tax Treatment |
|-----|------------|---------------|
| 14 | Employment income | Fully taxable. Total of all employment income before deductions. |
| 16 | Employee's CPP contributions | Non-refundable tax credit at 15% federal rate. |
| 17 | Employee's QPP contributions | Non-refundable tax credit at 15% (Quebec residents). |
| 18 | Employee's EI premiums | Non-refundable tax credit at 15% federal rate. |
| 20 | RPP contributions | Deductible from income. Reduces RRSP contribution room via pension adjustment. |
| 22 | Income tax deducted | Applied as a credit against total tax payable. If more than tax owed, the excess is refunded. |
| 24 | EI insurable earnings | Capped at the maximum insurable earnings for the year. Used to calculate EI benefits if needed. |
| 26 | CPP/QPP pensionable earnings | Capped at the YMPE for the year. Basis for CPP/QPP contribution calculation. |
| 40 | Taxable allowances and benefits | Included in Box 14. Represents employer-provided taxable benefits. |
| 42 | Employment commissions | Included in Box 14. Commission employees may deduct certain employment expenses. |
| 44 | Union dues | Deductible from income (Line 21200 on T1). |
| 46 | Charitable donations | Eligible for donation tax credit (federal: 15% on first $200, 29%/33% on remainder). |
| 52 | Pension adjustment | Reduces RRSP contribution room for the following year. |
| 55 | Employee's PPIP premiums | Provincial parental insurance (Quebec). |
| 56 | PPIP insurable earnings | Maximum insurable earnings for PPIP. |

### Other Important Boxes
| Box | Description |
|-----|------------|
| 10 | Province of employment | Determines which provincial tax rates apply. |
| 12 | Social Insurance Number (SIN) | Employee's SIN for identification. |
| 28 | Exempt CPP/QPP | Code indicating CPP/QPP exemption reason. |
| 29 | Employment code | Indicates type of employment (e.g., 11 = regular, 12 = seasonal). |
| 45 | Election under section 217 | Non-residents electing under section 217. |

## Taxable Benefits (Box 40)
Employer-provided benefits that are taxable must be included in the employee's income:
- Personal use of employer-provided automobile (standby charge + operating benefit)
- Group term life insurance premiums paid by employer (over $25,000 coverage)
- Employer-paid parking (over reasonable commuting benefit)
- Stock option benefits (when options are exercised)
- Board, lodging, and housing benefits
- Interest-free or low-interest employee loans (imputed interest at prescribed rate)
- Tuition paid by employer for personal courses (non-employment-related)
- Gift cards and near-cash gifts exceeding $500/year

### Non-Taxable Benefits (Not Included in Box 14)
- Employer contributions to a registered pension plan (RPP)
- Employer-paid private health insurance premiums
- Reasonable uniform/special clothing allowances
- Subsidized meals at an employer-operated cafeteria (if employee pays a reasonable charge)
- Non-cash gifts and awards up to $500/year (excluding gift cards)
- Employer-paid professional membership dues required for the job
- Employer-paid moving expenses for employer-initiated relocations

## Pension Adjustment (Box 52)
- Represents the value of pension benefits accrued in the year under a registered pension plan (RPP) or deferred profit sharing plan (DPSP).
- Directly reduces the RRSP contribution room for the following year.
- Formula for RRSP room: 18% of prior year earned income - pension adjustment = new RRSP room (plus carryforward).
- A defined benefit pension plan typically generates a larger PA than a defined contribution plan with the same employer contribution.

## Multiple T4 Slips
- An employee may receive multiple T4 slips if they had multiple employers during the year.
- All employment income is combined on the T1 return.
- CPP and EI contributions from multiple employers may result in overpayment, which is refunded on the T1 return.
- The maximum CPP/EI contribution limits apply per individual per year, regardless of the number of employers.

## How Employment Income is Taxed
1. Box 14 (Employment income) is reported on Line 10100 of the T1 return.
2. Deductions are subtracted:
   - RPP contributions (Box 20) on Line 20700
   - Union dues (Box 44) on Line 21200
   - Employment expenses (if applicable, with T2200)
3. Net employment income contributes to total income (Line 15000).
4. RRSP deductions, other deductions applied to reach net income and taxable income.
5. Federal and provincial tax calculated on taxable income.
6. Tax credits (BPA, CPP, EI, etc.) reduce tax payable.
7. Box 22 (tax deducted at source) is applied as a credit. If Box 22 > total tax payable, the difference is refunded.

## Common T4 Scenarios
### Scenario: Employee with income below CPP/EI caps
- Box 14 = Box 24 = Box 26 (all the same amount)
- CPP = (income - $3,500) × rate
- EI = income × rate

### Scenario: Employee with income above CPP/EI caps
- Box 14 > Box 24 (capped at EI max insurable)
- Box 14 > Box 26 (capped at YMPE)
- CPP = maximum contribution for the year
- EI = maximum premium for the year

### Scenario: Employee starting mid-year
- Box 14 reflects only earnings for the employment period
- Box 26 shows actual pensionable earnings (not prorated YMPE)
- CPP exemption is prorated by month: $3,500 × (months worked / 12)
