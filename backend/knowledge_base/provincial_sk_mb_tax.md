## Saskatchewan Tax Credits and Benefits

### Saskatchewan Low-Income Tax Credit (SLITC)
- Refundable credit for low-income residents, delivered quarterly with GST/HST credit.
- 2024: Up to $384 per adult, $150 per child.
- Reduced by 2% of family net income over $38,047.

### Saskatchewan Graduate Retention Program
- Non-refundable credit up to $20,000 for recent post-secondary graduates who live and work in Saskatchewan.
- Claimed over up to 10 years ($2,000/year maximum).
- Must have graduated from an eligible institution after 2009.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Saskatchewan
- 2024-2025: $752 per adult, $376 per child (quarterly).
- Saskatchewan residents receive among the highest carbon rebates.

## Key Saskatchewan Tax Planning Considerations
1. Saskatchewan has only 3 brackets — simple progressive structure.
2. PST is 6% (separate from GST, not harmonized).
3. No health premium.
4. The Graduate Retention Program is a significant benefit for new graduates staying in SK.
5. Low overall tax burden compared to most provinces.

## Manitoba Tax Credits and Benefits

### Manitoba Personal Tax Credit
- Non-refundable credits calculated at the lowest rate (10.80%).
- Manitoba has its own fitness tax credit and green energy equipment credit.

### Manitoba Primary Caregiver Tax Credit
- Up to $1,400 refundable credit for individuals providing care to a family member at home.

### Manitoba Education Property Tax Credit
- Up to $1,100 in 2024 for principal residence property taxes.
- $700 for renters (20% of rent deemed to be property tax).
- Reduced for income over $24,587 at 2% rate.

### Manitoba Child Benefit
- Quarterly payments for families with children under 18.
- Delivered through the Canada Child Benefit system.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Manitoba
- 2024-2025: $600 per adult, $300 per child (quarterly).

## Key Manitoba Tax Planning Considerations
1. Manitoba's 2024 reforms (raised BPA to $15,780 and brackets to $47K/$100K) are the most significant provincial tax cuts in recent years.
2. Manitoba charges a health and post-secondary education tax levy (payroll tax) — employer-paid, not employee.
3. RST (Retail Sales Tax) is 7% in Manitoba (not harmonized with GST).
4. The Education Property Tax Credit helps offset high property taxes — claim it on your return.
5. Manitoba's top combined rate (50.40%) is moderate compared to other provinces.
