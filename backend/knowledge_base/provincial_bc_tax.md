## BC Tax Credits and Benefits

### BC Climate Action Tax Credit
- Refundable tax credit paid quarterly with GST/HST credit.
- 2024: Up to $504 per adult, $252 per child.
- Reduced by 2% of adjusted family net income over $41,071 (single) or $57,288 (family).
- Applied for automatically when filing a tax return.

### BC Sales Tax Credit
- Part of the Climate Action Tax Credit; combined delivery with GST/HST credit.
- Must file a tax return even if no income to receive payments.

### BC Renter's Tax Credit (New 2024)
- Starting for the 2023 tax year, BC introduced a refundable renter's tax credit.
- Up to $400 per year for eligible renters.
- Income-tested: reduced for family income above $60,000, eliminated above $80,000.
- Must have rented a principal residence in BC during the tax year.

### BC Training Tax Credit
- Refundable credit for apprentices completing technical training.
- Basic: $1,000/year during training; completion: $2,500 for Red Seal trades.

### BC Home Owner Grant
- Not a tax credit but a property tax reduction up to $770 (basic) or $1,045 (seniors/disabled).
- Income threshold for supplemental grant: adjusted income under $35,000.

### BC Medical Services Plan (MSP)
- As of January 1, 2020, BC eliminated MSP premiums. There is no longer a health premium in BC.

## Key BC Tax Planning Considerations
1. BC has 7 tax brackets — the most of any province — creating more bracket-straddling opportunities.
2. The top combined rate (53.50%) is among the highest in Canada, making RRSP contributions especially valuable for high earners.
3. BC has no provincial sales tax on most services and a 7% PST on goods (not harmonized with GST).
4. BC's Climate Action Tax Credit provides ongoing quarterly payments — file your return even with zero income.
5. The new Renter's Tax Credit (2024+) is a significant benefit for lower-income renters.
