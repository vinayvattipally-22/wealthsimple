# Quebec Provincial Income Tax
Source: Revenu Québec TP-1 Return, Quebec Tax Tables
Last Updated: 2025 Tax Year

## Important Note: Quebec Tax System
Quebec has its own provincial tax administration (Revenu Québec) separate from the CRA. Quebec residents file two returns: a federal return (T1) with the CRA and a provincial return (TP-1) with Revenu Québec. Quebec also has a 16.5% federal tax abatement — federal tax is reduced by 16.5% for Quebec residents.

## Quebec Tax Credits and Benefits

### Quebec Solidarity Credit (Crédit d'impôt pour solidarité)
- Combines three components: GST, housing (rent/property tax), and northern villages.
- 2024: Up to $1,032 per adult + housing component up to $756.
- Paid monthly by Revenu Québec. File TP-1 return with Schedule D to apply.
- Reduced based on family net income above $37,645 (single).

### Quebec Work Premium (Prime au travail)
- Refundable credit for low-income workers (similar to federal CWB).
- 2024: Up to $1,185 for single, $1,842 for couple.
- Income threshold: reduced when income exceeds $11,852 (single).
- Adapted Work Premium available for persons with disabilities.

### Quebec Childcare (Régie des services de garde)
- Quebec has a $8.85/day subsidized daycare system (2024).
- Families not in subsidized daycare can claim the refundable childcare tax credit on TP-1.
- Credit rate: 75% of eligible expenses (decreasing with income) for expenses up to $11,935 per child.

### Quebec Shield Tax Credit (Bouclier fiscal)
- Protects low/moderate income earners from losing benefits as income rises.
- Covers 75% of the increase in work premium and childcare credit reductions due to income growth.
- Income range: approximately $25,000–$60,000 adjusted family income.

### Quebec Pension Plan (QPP) vs CPP
- Quebec residents contribute to QPP instead of CPP.
- QPP employee rate is higher: 6.40% in 2024 (vs CPP 5.95%).
- QPP also has a QPP2 (enhanced) contribution similar to CPP2.

### Quebec Parental Insurance Plan (QPIP)
- Replaces EI maternity/parental benefits for Quebec residents.
- Premium rate: 0.494% (employee), 0.692% (employer) in 2024.
- Quebec EI rate is correspondingly lower (1.32% vs 1.66% for rest of Canada).

## Key Quebec Tax Planning Considerations
1. Quebec has the highest combined top marginal rate (53.31%) — RRSP contributions are especially valuable.
2. The 16.5% federal tax abatement partially offsets Quebec's higher provincial rates.
3. Quebec's Solidarity Credit is substantial — always file Schedule D with your TP-1 return.
4. Quebec has unique deductions not available in other provinces (QC union dues, QC-specific medical expenses).
5. Quebec residents file TWO returns — federal (T1) and provincial (TP-1).
6. Quebec's subsidized $8.85/day daycare significantly reduces childcare costs vs other provinces.
7. The 2023 tax rate reduction (15%→14%, 20%→19%) provided meaningful tax relief for Quebec residents.
