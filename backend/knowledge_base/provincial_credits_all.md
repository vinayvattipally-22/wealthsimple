# Provincial Tax Credits and Benefits — All Provinces Summary
Source: CRA Provincial Tax Guides, Provincial Government Websites
Last Updated: 2025 Tax Year

This document provides a comprehensive summary of key provincial tax credits and benefits available across all Canadian provinces, organized by province code.

---

## Ontario (ON)

### Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)
- Combines: OEPTC (up to $1,194), OSTC (up to $345/person), NOEC (up to $277 for northern residents).
- Monthly payments. File ON-BEN form with return. Reduced based on family income.

### Ontario LIFT Credit
- Up to $875 for low-income workers. Reduced by 10% of income over ~$32,000. Cannot reduce Ontario tax below zero.

### Ontario CARE Credit
- Refundable childcare credit. 75% of expenses for income up to $20,000; decreasing to 0% above $150,000.

### Ontario Health Premium
- $0 to $900 depending on taxable income. Not technically a tax credit — it's a levy collected with income tax.

### Ontario Seniors' Public Transit Tax Credit
- Refundable credit for seniors 65+ who purchase transit passes.

## British Columbia (BC)

### BC Climate Action Tax Credit
- Up to $504/adult, $252/child (2024). Paid quarterly with GST/HST credit. Reduced by 2% of family income over $41,071.

### BC Renter's Tax Credit (New 2024+)
- Up to $400/year for eligible renters. Reduced for family income above $60,000, eliminated above $80,000.

### BC Training Tax Credit
- $1,000/year for apprentices; $2,500 for Red Seal completion.

### BC Home Owner Grant
- Reduces property tax by up to $770 (basic) or $1,045 (seniors/disabled). Income-tested for supplemental amount.

### BC Sales Tax Credit
- Included in Climate Action Tax Credit delivery.

---

## Alberta (AB)

### Alberta Child and Family Benefit (ACFB)
- Tax-free quarterly payments. Up to $1,469/child (2024). Working component: $764 if earned income > $2,760. Reduced above $27,024 family income.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Alberta
- $772/adult, $386/child (2024-2025). Highest in Canada. 20% rural supplement.

### Alberta Seniors Benefit
- Up to $310/month for low-income seniors 65+. Income below $29,630.

---

## Saskatchewan (SK)

### Saskatchewan Low-Income Tax Credit (SLITC)
- $384/adult, $150/child (2024). Paid quarterly with GST/HST credit. Reduced by 2% of family income over $38,047.

### Graduate Retention Program
- Up to $20,000 non-refundable credit ($2,000/year for 10 years) for graduates working in SK.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Saskatchewan
- $752/adult, $376/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.

### Active Families Benefit
- $150/child for sport, culture, and recreation activities.

---

## Manitoba (MB)

### Manitoba Education Property Tax Credit
- Up to $1,100 for homeowners, $700 for renters. Reduced by 2% of income over $24,587.

### Manitoba Primary Caregiver Tax Credit
- Up to $1,400 refundable credit for home caregivers.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Manitoba
- $600/adult, $300/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.

### Manitoba Personal Tax Credit
- Non-refundable. Includes fitness credit and green energy equipment credits.

---

## Quebec (QC)

### Quebec Solidarity Credit
- Up to $1,032/adult + housing component ($756). Monthly payments. File Schedule D with TP-1.

### Quebec Work Premium (Prime au travail)
- Up to $1,185 (single), $1,842 (couple). Phase-in above $3,000 earned income.

### Quebec Shield Tax Credit (Bouclier fiscal)
- 75% coverage of benefit reductions caused by income growth. Range: ~$25,000–$60,000 adjusted income.

### Quebec Childcare Tax Credit
- 75% of eligible expenses (decreasing with income) for families not in subsidized $8.85/day daycare.

### Quebec TP-1 Specific Deductions
- Quebec has its own deduction and credit system — many differ from federal.
- Quebec union dues, QPP contributions, QPIP premiums all have Quebec-specific credit treatment.

---

## New Brunswick (NB)

### NB Low-Income Tax Reduction
- Reduces tax for individuals with net income below $22,071 (2024).

### NB Seniors' Home Renovation Tax Credit
- 10% refundable credit on up to $10,000 of eligible renovation costs.

### NB Child Tax Benefit
- Up to $250/child quarterly.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — New Brunswick
- $380/adult, $190/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.

---

## Nova Scotia (NS)

### NS Affordable Living Tax Credit
- $255/adult + $60/child. Paid quarterly with GST/HST credit.

### NS Poverty Reduction Credit
- $250/adult + $62.50/child for low-income residents.

### NS Graduate Scholarship
- 50% tuition reduction for graduates living/working in NS.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — Nova Scotia
- $352/adult, $176/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.

---

## Prince Edward Island (PE)

### PEI Sales Tax Credit
- $110/adult + $55/child for low-income residents.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — PEI
- $240/adult, $120/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.

---

## Newfoundland and Labrador (NL)

### NL Low-Income Tax Reduction
- Reduces provincial tax for income below $21,437 (2024).

### NL Seniors' Benefit
- Up to $1,444/year for low-income seniors 65+.

### NL Income Supplement
- $500/year for adults with income below $40,000.

### NL Home Heating Rebate
- Annual rebate for residential heating costs.

### Canada Carbon Rebate — NL
- $298/adult, $149/child (2024-2025). 20% rural supplement.
