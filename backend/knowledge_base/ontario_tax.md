## Ontario Surtax
Ontario levies a surtax on provincial tax exceeding certain thresholds:
- 20% of basic Ontario tax in excess of $4,991 (2024)
- 36% of basic Ontario tax in excess of $6,387 (2024)

The surtax thresholds are indexed annually. The surtax effectively increases the marginal rate for higher incomes.

## Ontario Tax Credits (ON479)

### Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)
Combines three credits into a single monthly payment:
1. **Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit (OEPTC)**: Up to $1,194 (2024) for property tax/rent paid. Reduced by 2% of adjusted family net income over $24,092 (single) or $30,114 (family).
2. **Northern Ontario Energy Credit (NOEC)**: Up to $180 for single, $277 for family (northern residents only).
3. **Ontario Sales Tax Credit (OSTC)**: Up to $345 per adult + $345 per child (2024). Reduced by 4% of adjusted family net income over $32,864 (single) or $41,080 (family).

### Ontario Low-Income Individuals and Families Tax (LIFT) Credit
- Provides up to $875 in Ontario tax relief for low-income workers (2024).
- Available if employment income is greater than $0.
- Reduced by 10% of individual net income over approximately $32,000, and eliminated above certain thresholds.
- Cannot reduce Ontario personal income tax below zero.

### Ontario Childcare Access and Relief from Expenses (CARE) Credit
- Refundable credit for childcare expenses.
- 75% of eligible expenses for family income up to $20,000, decreasing to 0% above $150,000.

## Ontario Health Premium
A separate levy based on taxable income (not on the tax return but collected with income tax):
| Taxable Income | Health Premium |
|---------------|---------------|
| $0 – $20,000 | $0 |
| $20,000 – $25,000 | 6% of income over $20,000 |
| $25,000 – $36,000 | $300 |
| $36,000 – $38,500 | $300 + 6% of income over $36,000 |
| $38,500 – $48,000 | $450 |
| $48,000 – $48,600 | $450 + 25% of income over $48,000 |
| $48,600 – $72,000 | $600 |
| $72,000 – $72,600 | $600 + 25% of income over $72,000 |
| $72,600 – $200,000 | $750 |
| $200,000 – $200,600 | $750 + 25% of income over $200,000 |
| Over $200,600 | $900 |
