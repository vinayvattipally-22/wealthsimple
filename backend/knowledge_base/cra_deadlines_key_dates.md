# CRA Key Deadlines and Important Tax Dates
Source: CRA Website, Income Tax Act
Last Updated: 2025 Tax Year

## Annual Tax Filing Deadlines

### T1 Individual Tax Return Filing
- **April 30**: Filing deadline for most individuals. This is the deadline to file your T1 return and pay any balance owing for the previous tax year.
- **June 15**: Extended filing deadline for self-employed individuals and their spouses. However, any balance owing is still due by April 30 — only the filing deadline is extended, NOT the payment deadline.
- **If the deadline falls on a weekend/holiday**: The deadline is extended to the next business day.

### Provincial Return (Quebec TP-1)
- Quebec residents must also file a TP-1 return with Revenu Québec by April 30 (or June 15 if self-employed).

## RRSP Contribution Deadline
- **First 60 days of the calendar year** (typically March 1, or February 29 in a leap year): Last day to make RRSP contributions that can be deducted on the previous year's tax return.
- Example: Contributions made between January 1 and March 1, 2025 can be deducted on either the 2024 or 2025 tax return.

## FHSA Contribution Deadline
- **December 31**: FHSA contributions must be made by December 31 of the calendar year to count for that year's deduction.
- Unlike RRSPs, there is no 60-day grace period for FHSAs.

## TFSA Contribution Timing
- **January 1**: New TFSA contribution room becomes available.
- **January 1**: Withdrawn amounts from the previous year are added back to contribution room.
- No filing deadline — contributions can be made at any time during the year.

## Quarterly Benefit Payment Dates (2025)
The following benefits are paid quarterly, typically on the 5th of the month:

### GST/HST Credit
- January 3, 2025
- April 4, 2025
- July 4, 2025
- October 3, 2025

### Canada Carbon Rebate (CCR)
- Paid with GST/HST credit on the same dates.

### Canada Child Benefit (CCB)
- Paid monthly, typically on the 20th of each month.
- Based on the previous year's tax return (July to June payment period).
- July 2025 payments are based on the 2024 tax return.

### Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)
- Monthly payments, starting July of the year after filing.
- Must file ON-BEN form with your return.

### Quebec Solidarity Credit
- Monthly payments from Revenu Québec.
- Must file Schedule D with TP-1.

## Instalment Payment Dates
If you owe more than $3,000 in net tax ($1,800 for Quebec) in the current year AND in either of the two preceding years, you must pay quarterly instalments:
- **March 15**
- **June 15**
- **September 15**
- **December 15**
Interest charges apply if instalments are missed or insufficient.

## Key Business Deadlines
- **T4 Slips**: Employers must issue T4 slips to employees by the last day of February.
- **T5 Slips**: Issuers must distribute T5 slips by the last day of February.
- **T3 Slips**: Trust income slips must be issued by the last day of March.

## Tax Reassessment and Objection Deadlines
- **Notice of Objection**: Must be filed within 90 days of the date on the Notice of Assessment or Reassessment.
- **CRA Reassessment Period**: Generally 3 years from the date of the original Notice of Assessment (6 years for non-arm's length transactions or certain other situations; no time limit for fraud).
- **Request for Adjustment (T1-ADJ)**: Can be filed for up to 10 previous tax years.

## RRSP Conversion Deadline
- **December 31 of the year you turn 71**: Final deadline to convert RRSP to RRIF or annuity. After this date, you cannot contribute to your own RRSP.

## Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) Repayment
- Repayments begin the **second year after the year of withdrawal**.
- Example: If you withdrew from RRSP under HBP in 2024, first repayment is due when filing the 2026 return (early 2027).
- Minimum repayment: 1/15th of the total withdrawal per year for 15 years.

## Lifelong Learning Plan (LLP) Repayment
- Repayments begin the **fifth year after the first withdrawal** (or earlier if you stop being a qualifying student).
- Annual repayment: 1/10th of the outstanding balance for 10 years.

## Important 2024-2025 Specific Dates
- **February 24, 2025**: RRSP contribution deadline for the 2024 tax year (first 60 days of 2025 ends March 3, which is a Monday, but Feb 24 is the recommended safe date). Actual deadline is March 3, 2025.
- **April 30, 2025**: T1 filing and payment deadline for 2024 tax year.
- **June 15, 2025 (falls on Sunday → June 16)**: Self-employed filing deadline.
- **December 31, 2025**: Last day for 2025 TFSA and FHSA contributions; last day for charitable donations to claim on 2025 return.

## Penalty for Late Filing
- **5% of the balance owing** + 1% per full month late (up to 12 months) = maximum 17% penalty.
- **Repeat offenders** (late filed in any of the 3 preceding years): 10% + 2% per month (up to 20 months) = maximum 50%.
- No penalty if no balance owing — but filing is still required to receive benefit payments.

## Interest on Unpaid Tax
- CRA charges compound daily interest on unpaid balances.
- The prescribed interest rate changes quarterly (Q1 2025: 8% for overdue taxes, 4% for refunds).
- Interest is NOT tax-deductible for individuals.
