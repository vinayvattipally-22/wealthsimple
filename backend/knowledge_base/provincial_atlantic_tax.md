## NB Tax Credits and Benefits
- **NB Low-Income Tax Reduction**: Reduces tax for individuals with income below $22,071 (2024).
- **NB Seniors' Home Renovation Tax Credit**: Refundable credit of 10% of eligible renovation costs up to $10,000.
- **NB Child Tax Benefit**: Up to $250 per child, delivered quarterly.
- **NB School Tax Rebate**: Rebates on property tax for principal residences.
- **HST**: New Brunswick uses harmonized HST at 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial).

## NS Tax Credits and Benefits
- **NS Affordable Living Tax Credit**: Up to $255 per adult + $60 per child (2024), delivered quarterly with GST/HST credit.
- **NS Poverty Reduction Credit**: $250 per adult + $62.50 per child for low-income residents.
- **NS Graduate Scholarship**: 50% of tuition reduction for graduates who live and work in NS.
- **NS Volunteer Firefighter/SAR Tax Credit**: $500 non-refundable credit.
- **HST**: Nova Scotia uses harmonized HST at 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial).

## Key NS Tax Consideration
Nova Scotia has the highest top combined marginal rate in Canada at 54.00% (federal 33% + NS 21%). RRSP deductions are extremely valuable for high-income NS residents.

## NL Tax Credits and Benefits
- **NL Low-Income Tax Reduction**: Reduces tax for low-income individuals (income below $21,437 in 2024).
- **NL Seniors' Benefit**: Up to $1,444 per year for low-income seniors 65+.
- **NL Income Supplement**: $500/year for adults with income below $40,000.
- **NL Home Heating Rebate**: Annual rebate for heating costs (amount varies by fuel type).
- **HST**: NL uses harmonized HST at 15% (5% federal + 10% provincial).
- **NL Temporary Gas Tax Relief**: Periodically reduces fuel taxes.

## Key NL Tax Consideration
Newfoundland has the highest top combined marginal rate in Canada at 54.30% (federal 33% + NL 21.30%) as of 2024, surpassing Nova Scotia. The 7-bracket system introduced in 2022 significantly increased taxes on high earners.
