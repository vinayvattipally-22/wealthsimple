## Alberta Tax Credits and Benefits

### Alberta Child and Family Benefit (ACFB)
- Tax-free quarterly payment for families with children under 18.
- Maximum benefit (2024): $1,469 per child (1 child), increasing per child count.
- Reduced when family income exceeds $27,024.
- Working component: Additional $764 per family if earned income exceeds $2,760.

### Alberta Climate Leadership Adjustment Rebate
- Replaced by federal carbon tax backstop. No separate provincial carbon rebate — Alberta residents receive the federal Canada Carbon Rebate (CCR).

### Canada Carbon Rebate (CCR) — Alberta
- 2024-2025: $772 per adult, $386 per child (quarterly payments).
- Alberta receives one of the highest rebates due to the federal carbon pricing backstop.

### Alberta Seniors Benefit
- Monthly non-taxable payment for low-income seniors aged 65+.
- Maximum: $310/month for single seniors with income below $29,630.

## Key Alberta Tax Planning Considerations
1. Alberta has the lowest starting rate at 10% flat for the first $148,269 — making it the most tax-friendly province for moderate incomes.
2. No provincial sales tax (no PST, no HST) — only 5% federal GST applies.
3. No health premium (unlike Ontario).
4. The top combined rate (48%) is the lowest of any province, making Alberta the best province for high earners.
5. Alberta's high BPA ($21,885 in 2024) means more income is shielded from provincial tax.
6. The ACFB is a significant benefit for families — file a return even with low income to receive it.
7. Alberta residents benefit significantly from the Canada Carbon Rebate — file annually to claim it.
